Here’s the complete version : 4-Day Hiking Tour: Hayat, Uhum &amp; Asraf with Village Homestays Day 1 – Arrival in Hayat Village 9:00 AM: Meet your driver at the tour start point and depart for Hayat Village. Arrive around lunchtime and be warmly welcomed by your local host. Settle into your homestay and relax. Afternoon: Take a short walk through the village and visit a nearby breeding enclosure protecting the endangered Severtsov’s wild sheep. Hike up the hill opposite the enclosure to see abandoned houses from the 1950s Soviet resettlements. Continue into Hayat Valley, observing the region’s diverse flora and fauna and enjoying the lush landscape, especially in spring. Evening: Return to the guesthouse and enjoy a relaxing evening wandering around the village. Day 2 – Hike to Uhum Village Morning: After breakfast, meet your mountain guide and begin the hike to Uhum Village via Upper Ukhum (12 km / approx. 4 hours). From the mountain pass, enjoy panoramic views of the village below. In Upper Ukhum , see ancient irrigation channels still in use and visit a historic petroglyph site before returning to Hayat Village. Afternoon: Relax at the guesthouse and take a gentle walk around the village, soaking in local life. Day 3 – Hike to Asraf Village Morning: After breakfast, prepare for your hike to Asraf Village. Your mountain guide will lead the way while your luggage is transported separately. Trek through Ukhum Valley to Asraf (11 km / approx. 4.5 hours). Along the way, visit the old community watermill, still in operation using a hydro pump without electricity, and watch locals demonstrate its workings. Continue to Asraf Village. Afternoon: Upon arrival, your host will welcome you and show you the homestay facilities, followed by lunch. Spend a relaxing afternoon exploring the village or observing local life. Evening: Participate in preparing a traditional Plov using homegrown vegetables and mountain spices alongside your host family. Day 4 – Hike to Eski Forish and Return to Samarkand Morning: After breakfast, embark on a guided hike along the buffer zone of the Nuratau Nature Reserve to Eski Forish Village (10 km / 3–4 hours round trip). Enjoy breathtaking views of Lake Aydar and the Kyzylkum steppe. Along the way, discover ancient petroglyphs and visit the ruins of a medieval lookout once used to monitor the plains against nomadic raids. Afternoon: Return to Asraf Village for lunch, then transfer to Samarkand to conclude your tour.
