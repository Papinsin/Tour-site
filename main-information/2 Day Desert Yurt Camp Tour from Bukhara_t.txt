2 Day Desert Yurt Camp Tour from Bukhara Day 1: Bukhara – Nurata – Yurt Camp After breakfast in Bukhara, embark on a three-hour drive to Nurata , a town rich in history. Visit the Chashma Complex, known for its sacred spring, explore Nur Fortress, built by Alexander the Great, and admire Juma Mosque. Enjoy a traditional Uzbek lunch before heading on a two-hour drive to a yurt camp in the Kyzylkum Desert. Upon arrival, settle into your traditional yurt, then take a camel ride or enjoy a desert walk. As the sun sets, experience an authentic Uzbek dinner followed by a folk music performance around a campfire under a starlit sky. Spend the night in a peaceful yurt stay, immersed in the silence of the desert. Day 2: Yurt Camp – Lake Aydarkul – Samarkand Wake up to a desert sunrise and enjoy breakfast before heading to Lake Aydarkul . Spend the morning relaxing by the lake, swimming, or birdwatching, then enjoy a picnic lunch featuring fresh fish. After lunch, drive three hours to Samarkand, arriving in the late afternoon. Check in to your hotel and unwind, or explore the city at your own pace. For dinner, consider a meal at Platan Restaurant, known for its modern Uzbek cuisine. Day 2 – Sentob Village: Kadvan or Fazilman Hike Morning: After breakfast, meet your mountain guide for a full-day hike.
