## 1. 2 Day Desert Yurt Camp Tour from Bukhara — **2 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-01.png`

**Day 1: Bukhara – Nurata – Yurt Camp**  
After breakfast in Bukhara, 3-hour drive to Nurata. Visit Chashma Complex (sacred spring), Nur Fortress (Alexander the Great), Juma Mosque. Traditional Uzbek lunch. 2-hour drive to yurt camp in Kyzylkum Desert. Settle into yurt, camel ride or desert walk. Uzbek dinner, folk music by campfire. Overnight in yurt.

**Day 2: Yurt Camp – Lake Aydarkul – Samarkand**  
Desert sunrise, breakfast. Head to Lake Aydarkul: relax, swim, birdwatch, picnic lunch (fresh fish). 3-hour drive to Samarkand. Check in, optional dinner at Platan Restaurant.

**Included:** Accommodation, full board (breakfast, lunch, dinner), transportation.  
**Price:** from **170 euro** per person.

**Variant (Option A – from Samarkand):**  
Day 1: Samarkand – Nurata – Yurt camp (Chashma, Alexander’s fortress, local lunch, yurt camp, short camel ride, Kazakh akyn performance). Day 2: Yurt camp – Aydarkul – Bukhara (breakfast, lakeside picnic, drive to Bukhara, Rabat-i-Malik caravanserai, Gijduvan pottery workshop). End of services.

## 2. 2-Day Nuratau Mountain Horse Riding Tour — **2 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-02.png`

**Day 1 – Bukhara → Hayat Village**  
09:00 Meet driver in Bukhara, 4.5–5 h drive to Hayat. Lunch at Guesthouse. Meet guide, test ride and basic instructions. Afternoon: 2-hour horse ride to Upper Uhum (stone/mud houses, ancient fortress, village life). Overnight: Guesthouse. Meals: L, D.

**Day 2 – Hayat → Uhum → Asraf → Samarkand**  
Breakfast. 12 km ride Hayat to Asraf (~3 h), views of Lake Aydarkul and Kyzyl-Kum steppe. Asraf Guesthouse. Afternoon: 2-hour ride to Eski Forish. Driver transfer to Samarkand (~4 h). Overnight: Samarkand hotel. Meals: B, L.

**Included:** Accommodation (Guesthouses), 1 breakfast, 2 lunches, 1 dinner (veg/vegan possible), local horse guide Day 1–2, transport Bukhara → Asraf, Uhum → Samarkand, horse rental.  
**Not included:** Personal expenses, travel insurance (required).  
**Price:** **€550** total (e.g. 2 × €275).

## 3. 3-Day Rural Homestay in Nuratau Mountain Villages (Hayat) — **3 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-03.png`

**Day 1: Bukhara – Nuratau (Hayat)**  
09:00 Depart Bukhara. 12:00 Nurata: Alexander’s fortress ruins, Suzani workshop, Chashma Mosque and spring. 13:00 Continue to Hayat (220 km, ~4 h). 15:00 Arrive, homestay check-in. 15:30 Lunch. 16:30–18:00 Village walk: Severtsov’s sheep enclosure, old settlement ruins. 18:00 Dinner. **Day 3: Hayat – Samarkand**  
08:00 Breakfast. 09:00 Depart Hayat. 13:00 Arrive Samarkand.

**Price:** **500 euro** (e.g. 2 × €250).

## 5. 3-Day Yurt Stay, Hiking & Lake Aydarkul — **3 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-05.png`

**Day 1 – Bukhara → Nurata, Lake Aydarkul, Desert Yurt Camp**  
09:00 Scenic 5 h drive to yurt camp via Kyzylkum. Nurata: Alexander’s fortress, sacred spring, mosque. Lunch at Lake Aydarkul (walk/swim). Afternoon: yurt camp, short camel ride, campfire dinner and local bard. Overnight: Yurt camp.

**Day 2 – Yurt Camp → Nurata Mountains, Hayat Village**  
Breakfast, transfer to Hayat. Stop: ancient tree (Alexander’s soldiers), mosque. Lunch at Hayat homestay. Afternoon: Severtsov’s sheep enclosure, Soviet-era abandoned houses, Hayat Valley. Evening at guesthouse. Overnight: Hayat homestay.

**Day 3 – Hayat → Samarkand**  
Breakfast, depart. ~4 h drive (220 km) to Samarkand. End of tour.

**Included:** 1 night yurt camp (private yurt 2–4 pax), 1 B, 1 L, 1 D (veg/vegan possible), transfers Bukhara–Yurt camp–Samarkand, 20 min camel ride, evening bard performance, Nurata entrance fees. English-speaking driver (no guide).  
**Not included:** Travel insurance (recommended), tips (€5–10/person/day suggested).  
**Price:** **250 euro** per person.

## 6. 4-Day Sentob Village Homestay & Nuratau Mountains Hike — **4 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-06.png`

**Day 1 – Bukhara → Sentob**  
09:00 Drive to Sentob. Host family, homestay (fruit gardens, tapchans). Lunch. Afternoon: mosque, fortress ruins, stone houses, village life. Evening with hosts, dinner. Overnight: Sentob homestay.

**Day 2 – Sentob: Kadvan or Fazilman**  
Breakfast, mountain guide. Choice: Kadvan Valley 15 km (~7.5 h) or Fazilman Plateau 22 km (~10 h). Sentob Valley: watermill, fortress, Arabic inscriptions, streams, waterfalls, pools. Picnic lunch. Birdlife. Return Sentob. Dinner, overnight.

**Day 3 – Sentob → Majerum**  
Breakfast. Hike to Majerum (11 km, 3.5–4 h), Nuratau Nature Reserve buffer. Homestay (e.g. Mr. Qoldosh, Ms. Muhabbat Mirzaeva). Afternoon: 2 h hike to legendary arbor vitae tree, mosque. Dinner, overnight Majerum.

**Day 4 – Majerum → Samarkand**  
Breakfast, goodbye. Drive to Samarkand. End.

**Price:** **270 euro** per person.

## 8. 4-Day Hiking Tour: Hayat, Uhum & Asraf (Village Homestays) — **4 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-08.png`

**Day 1 – Hayat Village**  
09:00 Transfer to Hayat. Lunch at homestay. Afternoon: Severtsov’s sheep enclosure, Soviet resettlement houses, Hayat Valley. Evening at guesthouse.

**Day 2 – Hayat → Uhum**  
Breakfast, mountain guide. Hike to Uhum via Upper Ukhum (12 km, ~4 h). Pass views, irrigation channels, petroglyphs. Return Hayat. Afternoon: village walk.

**Included:** 3 nights homestays, 3 B, 4 L, 3 D (veg/vegan on request), mountain guide Day 2–4, Bukhara–Hayat, Asraf–Samarkand.  
**Not included:** Personal expenses, travel insurance (recommended), tips (€5–10/person/day).  
**Price:** **300 euro** per person.

## 11. 7-Day Nuratau Mountain Hiking & Homestay — **7 days**

**Image:** `images/tours/tour-11.png`

**Day 1 – Bukhara → Sentob**  
09:00 Bukhara → Sentob. Homestay (fruit gardens, tapchans). Lunch. Explore village: mosque, fortress ruins, stone houses. Dinner with host family. Overnight: Sentob homestay.

**Day 2 – Sentob: Kadvan or Fazilman**  
Breakfast, mountain guide. Kadvan Valley 15 km (7.5–8 h) or Fazilman Plateau 22 km (~10 h). Streams, waterfalls, pools, picnic, birdlife. Return Sentob. Dinner, overnight. *Spring: routes may change (water levels).*

**Day 3 – Sentob → Majerum**  
Breakfast. Hike to Majerum (11 km, 3.5–4 h), Nuratau Nature Reserve buffer. Homestay. Afternoon: 2 h hike to legendary Juniper tree, mosque. Dinner, overnight: Majerum.

**Day 4 – Majerum → Lake Aydarkul → Hayat**  
09:00 Vehicle to Hayat. Stop Lake Aydarkul: swim, walk, birdlife (pelicans, cranes, ducks, geese). Hayat: host Parda Musaev (reserve warden). Homestay (6 rooms, carpets, kupacha). Afternoon: Severtsov’s sheep enclosure, Shaxi Korgon Fortress ruins. Garden by stream. Dinner, overnight: Hayat.

**Day 5 – Hayat → Uhum**  
Breakfast, guide. Hike to Upper Uhum (12 km, Guzar pass ~3.5 h one way). Views Karaul Peak, Hayat. Local life: tandir bread, carpets, kurpacha. Irrigation canals, petroglyphs. Picnic. Homestay Uhum. Dinner, overnight.

**Day 6 – Uhum → Asraf**  
Breakfast. Hike to Asraf (11 km, ~4 h). Luggage by donkey. Ukhum Valley, old watermill (hydro pump), Mushibirion Valley, Mongol fortress Qorgoni Honkeldi. Lunch with host. Afternoon: village. Evening: help cook plov. Overnight: Asraf.

**Day 7 – Asraf → Samarkand**  
Breakfast. Morning hike buffer zone to Eski Forish (11 km round trip, ~4 h). Lake Aydar, Kyzylkum steppe, petroglyphs, medieval lookout. Return Asraf, farewell lunch. Transfer to Samarkand. End of tour.

**Included:** 6 nights homestays, 6 B, 7 L, 6 D (veg/vegan possible), guided hikes Day 2–7, transfers Bukhara → Sentob, Sentob → Hayat via Lake Aydarkul, Asraf → Samarkand.  
**Price:** **€1,260** (e.g. 2 × €630).

## Horse Riding – General Info (applies to 2–6 day horse tours)

- **Beginner-friendly:** Yes; test ride and basic instructions on Day 1.  
- **Age:** 14+; under 18 with adult.  
- **Fitness:** Basic; daily rides 2.5–5 h.  
- **Health:** No serious back/joint/mobility issues.  
- **Weight limit:** 100 kg (220 lbs) max.  
- **Not included:** Personal expenses, travel insurance (required).
